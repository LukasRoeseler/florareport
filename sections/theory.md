## The Replication and Reproduction Crises

- Overview of the replication crisis in psychology [@osc2015reproducibility; @camerer2016economics] and the broader credibility revolution [@vazire2018credibility; @korbmacher2023replication].

## Defining Replications and Reproductions

- Definitions following @parsons2022glossary 
- FLoRA inclusion criteria: replications collect new data; reproductions re-analyse the original data.

## Tracking Replications

why
- replications facilitate cumulative science, inform robustness and generalizability
- replication attempts not linked to original studies (only via citation)
- large-scale projects are not citing them like meta-analyses do (e.g., RPP, SCORE)
- journals do not adhere to the pottery-barn rule (Reed et al., 2025)

history of different tracking approaches
- PsychFileDrawer (not exclusive to replications, offline now)
- CurateScience database, lots is based on PsychFileDrawer
- FORRT Replications and Reversals (unsystematic, crowdsourced list, also including meta-analyses and comments)
- Replication Database started with OSF Registries and large-scale projects such as R:PP and FORRT Replication Database, FReD 2.0 for SCORE
- The Replication Network
- Replication Wiki
- The Institute for Replication's working papers
